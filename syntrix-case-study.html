01 / CASE STUDY

A mismatch between qualifications and readiness

A résumé describes qualifications and experience. It does not always reveal how someone approaches unfamiliar problems, collaborates, adapts or responds to workplace situations.

At the same time, students and graduates need clearer insight into their strengths, skill gaps and career direction. Syntrix explored an opportunity at the intersection of HR-Tech and EdTech.

What if recruitment and career readiness focused on understanding a person's potential, rather than relying only on what their résumé says?

02 / CASE STUDY

Three audiences, connected needs

The team conducted direct interviews with recruitment professionals and students. The concept was also evaluated for career centres and educational institutions.

My contribution: collaborative research, product thinking and business-model development as part of a five-student Industrial Design team at VIT Vellore.

More efficient screening, behavioural insight and structured information to support recruitment decisions.

USER SEGMENT

USER SEGMENT

Structured career-readiness assessments and insights to support counselling and development.

04 / CASE STUDY

The proposed product — PWAT

Personalized Work and Skill Assessment Test (PWAT) is a proposed digital platform for scenario-based evaluations, connecting candidate assessment with career development.

Cognitive ability

Problem-solving, information processing and decision-making.

ASSESSMENT DIMENSION

Emotional intelligence

Emotional awareness and responses to interpersonal situations.

ASSESSMENT DIMENSION

Communication, collaboration and group challenges.

ASSESSMENT DIMENSION

ASSESSMENT DIMENSION

Responding to unfamiliar situations and changing requirements.

ASSESSMENT DIMENSION

Workplace-inspired situations tailored to an assessment context.

05 / CASE STUDY

The assessment experience

The proposed experience was designed as a six-step web-based journey. This was a business concept, not a fully deployed and validated workflow.

USER EXPERIENCE

For individuals

USER EXPERIENCE

For organisations

06 / CASE STUDY

A dual-market business model

The business plan explored a B2B and B2C model, with institutional partnerships as a bridge between the two.

CUSTOMER + CHANNEL

B2B · Organisations

  • Medium and large organisations
  • HR departments and recruitment agencies
  • Startups and small businesses
  • Educational and career institutions

Proposed channels: LinkedIn outreach, demonstrations, free trials, pilot proposals and partnerships.

CUSTOMER + CHANNEL

B2C · Individuals

  • Students and graduates
  • Job seekers
  • Early-career professionals

Proposed channels: campus drives, social campaigns, workshops, webinars and career clinics.

08 / CASE STUDY

What the team developed

A structured startup proposal combining research, product definition and business-model thinking.

  • B2B and B2C customer segmentation.
  • Assessment framework and proposed user journey.
  • Competitor review including SHL, Mercer, Mettl and MBTI.
  • Proposed pricing, revenue and go-to-market strategy.
  • Financial projections, operations, SWOT and sustainability analysis.

The team's Business Model Wheel self-assessment scored 90/100 (Offering 32/35, Monetisation 28/30, Sustainability 30/35). This is an internal evaluation, not market validation or proof of product-market fit.
