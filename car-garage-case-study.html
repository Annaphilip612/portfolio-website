Car Garage
Playset.

A mentor-led concept project I contributed to during my Product Design Internship at Funskool. I explored the market, play mechanisms and two distinct directions for a garage playset.

Project context
This was my mentor's project at Funskool. I contributed to the exploration and development work as an intern; I am presenting my involvement as part of the wider team process, not claiming sole authorship.
01 / THE BRIEF

Make a garage feel like more than parking.

The exploration looked at how a car garage playset could create a sequence of small, satisfying play moments — movement, surprises, pretend play and repeatable actions.

The supplied concept pitch explored an age group of 5+ and two directions: a linear play experience and a more compact soft-play configuration. Both aimed to combine several play features into one coherent toy system.

02 / MARKET SCAN

What was already out there?

The market review compared existing garage playsets on price, mechanisms and the kind of experience they offered.

PLAYBOX INDIA

“Drive-In” Wooden Garage

Winding-crank elevator and helipad; the review noted the absence of a standout “wow” centrepiece.

₹2,800–₹8,750
KIDOLOGY

3-Level Electronic Garage

LED lights, sounds and a battery elevator; the review described the design as generic.

₹2,788–₹5,999
JOYFLEET

5-Level Dual Lift Garage

Manual and magnetic dual lifts, but the experience still centred mainly on adding levels.

₹1,790–₹2,189

Price ranges and competitor observations are transcribed from the supplied market-research presentation; they are a historical snapshot, not current retail pricing.

03 / TWO DIRECTIONS

Linear play vs. soft play.

Rather than committing to a single layout too early, the concept pitch explored two different ways of arranging the play experience.

04 / PLAY FEATURES

Small mechanisms. Big play value.

The linear concept included a start and finish, car wash, fuel-up launching station, flying bridge and push-to-pop platform.

Flick-open barricade

A simple action to open and close the track barrier.

Airbag & water squirters

Surprise elements integrated into the play sequence.

Fuel-up & launcher

A pretend-play stop paired with a launch interaction and ramp.

Ferris-wheel lift & bridge

A vertical movement feature connected to a flying bridge and push/pop platform.

05 / EXTRA PLAY POSSIBILITIES

Make it personal. Make it repeatable.

The pitch also explored optional add-on play moments that could extend the toy beyond the core garage layout.

A magic paint shop — change the look of the car.
A stickering shop — customise the vehicle.
A wheel-swap shop — switch wheels and transform the ride.
